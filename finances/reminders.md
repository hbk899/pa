# Reminders

Things for Claude to proactively raise in chat when the timing is
relevant — checked against today's date at the start of a conversation
or whenever finances come up. Add new reminders anytime, any cadence.

## Recurring

| Name | Cadence | Trigger window | Reminder | Notes |
|------|---------|------------------|----------|-------|
| Rent cheque cover | Monthly | 2 days before due date through due date (i.e. 3rd–5th) | Check CBQ Current account has at least 3800 QAR before the 5th, when the rent cheque clears | Bounced cheque = fine. Balance as of last snapshot: see `finances/accounts.md` |
| CBQ credit card statement | Monthly | 2 days before through statement date (22nd–24th) | Review the CBQ Credit Mastercard statement and outstanding balance before it closes on the 24th | Limit 15,000 QAR. See `finances/liabilities.md` |
